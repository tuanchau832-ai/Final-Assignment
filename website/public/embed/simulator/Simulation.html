How to read the result

Any failed limit counts as one breached sample. Samples 1–2 show a yellow warning with alert = 0. Sample 3 confirms red with alert = 1. A sample within all limits resets the count and alert.

Report rule (QCVN 08:2023/BTNMT): pH 6.0–8.5, DO ≥ 4 mg/L, turbidity no more than 50% above the station baseline. Temperature and TDS are logged, not alarmed.

Team rule: adds TDS ≤ 500 ppm and temperature ≤ 35 °C; turbidity limit 75 NTU when DO ≥ 4.20 and TDS ≤ 400, otherwise 50 NTU.

This sensor combination is a project heuristic. It cannot establish the cause of turbidity or certify water safety.
