Appendix E — Ba Nguyen cost model

Analyst estimates; no vendor confirmation, actual funding or field acceptance is claimed. Checked 28 November 2026.

Authority and interpretation

The user authorised internal planning estimates without vendor confirmation. Listing x assumed quantity is distinct from a supplier quotation. All unquoted labour, site, tax, incident and disposal figures are explicitly analyst allowances. This is a complete model for the defined scope, not a verified real project price or an assurance of performance.

Financial totals

Base CAPEX 91,660,428 VND; OPEX 143,606,000 VND; lifecycle 47,495,976 VND. Add 56,552,481 VND general contingency for a modelled total of 339,314,885 VND. Low/high 260,250,806 / 468,154,608 VND are alternative assumptions, not confidence bounds or a spending ceiling.

Why the total exceeds the earlier hardware subtotal

The earlier 43,482,944 VND covers only three core stations and one shared kit. The previous 57,466,944 /70,398,864 VND figures add only reference components, starter supplies and selected service portions. They are included where applicable in the new model; do not add any of those overlapping totals to it.

Labour benchmark and selected rates

FDI Care describes skilled electrical work at450,000–600,000 VND/day; Vien Thong Tia Sang describes professional electricians at500,000–800,000 VND/day. The base600,000 VND/person-day (8h), 450,000 low and800,000 high are analyst choices informed by these other-industry benchmarks, not water-sensor quotations or a measured market average.

Monthly routes and calibration

The estimate pays 36 shared monthly route-days with two technicians, not only six visits. Six testing rounds and 16 station checks are included. It covers 96 pH tasks, monthly DO cleaning and 48 early two-monthly tasks each for DO/turbidity. Every two months is earlier than 90 days; it is an internal planning policy, not a new teammate commitment. The older independent 32-task calendar is not added to 48.

Consumables and no double charging

One initial buffer pair and one KCl bottle are already in startup stock. Eleven more buffer pairs and 23 KCl bottles give 12 quarterly pairs and 24 KCl bottles in total. Quantities are assumptions; supplier expiry and the actual immersion vessel override this model. Crew labour already includes calibration: no separate 150,000 per-task service charge is added.

Supply taxes and freight

The landed-cost line reserves 10% of unclear-tax supplies plus assumed freight; 5/20% form the low/high assumptions. This is not a legal VAT/duty rate or a classification of the imported products. Clearly VAT-included goods are excluded from that reserve. All other analyst unit allowances are all-in budget allowances, not invoices.

Cloud and data allowance

The shared monthly model includes web+Postgres compute USD 13, assumed 20 GB storage at USD 0.30/GB-month (USD6), and a separate USD 6 internal traffic/backup/service-fee reserve. USD 25 total is not a Render package. Capacity/retention are untested; the model assumes 30-day raw retention plus aggregates. It does not promise three years of every raw sample or cover an enterprise SLA.

Renewal reserves

Annual DO film-renewal dates before project close give six possible events. Because no exact compatible cap price is available, the budget conservatively reserves the listed full SEN0680 cost per event. It does not claim the sensor must be replaced annually. Six pH-kit replenishment allowances preserve the initial shared spare; one battery, one Q190 and one turbidity spare are condition-based funding pools. No replacement is ordered or failure predicted.

Power and engineering limits

The 100 W panel/12V40Ah AGM/buck5V combination is a costing scenario. Charger settings, peak current, protection, mounting and ingress performance remain untested. At an assumed 4 W average, 50% usable depth and 85% delivery efficiency yield 51 nominal hours; this is not measured autonomy. The 12 V AGM is not connected as a cell to the board charger. No motorised wiper, river civil works, confined-space entry, diving or boat work is included.

Payment and affordability proposal

A municipal or institutional pilot fund is proposed to pay equipment, integration and training. A funded operating contract is proposed for staff, services, consumables, replacements and collection. No household monitoring fee, confirmed grant, revenue or unpaid student labour is assumed. These are proposed financial roles, not assignments to classmates or named institutions. Expansion is deferred if sustainable recurring funding is unavailable.

Staging and allocations

Base startup CAPEX: phase1 37,023,247 VND, phase2 54,637,181 VND, before general contingency. Stations close at 36/30/30 months. The station allocation divides CAPEX/lifecycle equally and OPEX by36/30/30 exposure; it is bookkeeping allocation, not site quotations.
