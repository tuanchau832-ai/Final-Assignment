3 · Cost analysis

Staged costs and affordability

Basis and scope

Capital expenditure (CAPEX) covers equipment and setup; operating expenditure (OPEX) covers services while active. Q190 core equipment is 13,933,146 VND per station, or 43,482,944 VND for three stations and one optional shared pH kit. These hardware subtotals exclude the remaining pilot costs. The budget uses a historical 25,940 VND/USD assumption, not a current transaction rate (DFRobot, n.d.-a, n.d.-c; Hshop, n.d.-c; LILYGO, n.d.; NDH, n.d.).

The 36-month project starts one station before two more at illustrative month six. The 100W/40Ah power configuration and unquoted items are planning assumptions. Listed prices, market benchmarks and internal allowances are distinguished in Appendix E; simulated results do not establish field performance.

Affordability and payment proposal

A proposed municipal or institutional pilot fund would cover setup. Operations and renewal average about 5.31 million VND per project-month before contingency. This proposal charges households no monitoring fee and assumes no grant, income or unpaid labour. These are proposed funding roles, not commitments; income evidence does not establish affordability. If recurring funding is insufficient, retain one pilot and defer expansion.

Ordering the next two stations requires acceptance and an updated budget. Field power, coverage and repair capacity remain untested; the 48-hour response target is not a contracted service. Contingency does not guarantee performance or unlimited incident coverage.

4 · Risks and community reaction

What could go wrong, and the response

PossibleHigh impact
Readings near the limit are wrong

Listed error is about ±50 NTU for turbidity and ±0.6 mg/L for oxygen (DFRobot, n.d.-b, n.d.-c). Test against reference instruments before buying more.

LikelyMedium impact
Probe fouling and drift

Wiper brush, scheduled cleaning and an extra visit when readings drift.

PossibleHigh impact
Alert fatigue

Test the three-reading rule against the site baseline. Verify readings near the limit with a reference instrument.

PossibleMedium impact
Theft or flooding

Locked cage above the flood line, with ward staff watching the site.

PossibleHigh impact
Factory pushback

Laboratory confirmation before any fine; clusters can use alerts to fix their own treatment.

PossibleMedium impact
Digital divide

SMS and loudspeaker alerts, large text and Vietnamese labels.

What a working station changes

An illustrative scenario: Mrs Lan, 58, farms fish downstream on the Nhuệ in Thanh Trì.

02:00Discharge

Untreated water enters the river upstream of her ponds.

02:01Detected

A station sees oxygen fall for three readings in a row.

02:02Warned

She receives an SMS: do not pump river water tonight.

06:00Protected

She keeps the pond gates shut and her fish survive.

7 · Recommendations

What should happen next

Confirm quotes, operator and payer

Fix the final parts list and who runs and pays for it before ordering; budgeted allowances do not establish funding commitments.

Prove one station in the field

Test accuracy, fouling and battery life at one outfall before buying more.

Agree the legal role of the data

Work with the city on how alerts and laboratory results are used as evidence.

Test with older residents

Check SMS and loudspeaker alerts with people least likely to use the app.

Add ammonium sensing

Ammonium points directly to sewage, which the current probes show only indirectly.

Share and link the data

Publish quality-labelled data and combine it with the city’s river stations.
