1 · Rollout plan

Prove one station, then add two

Before launch

Survey and quote

  • Survey proposed sites on the Tô Lịch near Cầu Mới, Cầu Dậu and Cầu Thanh Liệt.
  • Get matching quotes for a complete installed station.
  • Agree site access and alert steps.
Station 1

Install and test

  • Run a two-week baseline.
  • Check readings against reference instruments.
  • Measure power use and 4G recovery.
Stations 2 and 3

Expand if it passes

  • Order only after the first station is accepted and the new quote approved.
  • Reuse the tested design and alert steps.
To year 3

Operate and decide

  • Measure detection time, false alarms and app use.
  • Review wear in month 36.
  • Decide on wider rollout from the results.

2 · Operation

Who runs it and keeps it working

Proposed roles. Each one still needs a written agreement.

Department of Agriculture and Environment

Owns the stations and data, receives alerts and sends inspectors.

System operator

Runs the platform and repairs stations. A main operator and a backup are still to be named.

Accredited laboratory

Tests the confirmation sample that supports any fine.

Wards and residents

Share alerts locally, report pollution with photos and help watch the sites.

4 · Risks and community reaction

What could go wrong, and the response

PossibleHigh impact
Readings near the limit are wrong

Listed error is about ±50 NTU for turbidity and ±0.6 mg/L for oxygen (DFRobot, n.d.-b, n.d.-c). Test against reference instruments before buying more.

LikelyMedium impact
Probe fouling and drift

Wiper brush, scheduled cleaning and an extra visit when readings drift.

PossibleHigh impact
Alert fatigue

Three-reading rule and a baseline for each site keep false alarms rare.

PossibleMedium impact
Theft or flooding

Locked cage above the flood line, with ward staff watching the site.

PossibleHigh impact
Factory pushback

Laboratory confirmation before any fine; clusters can use alerts to fix their own treatment.

PossibleMedium impact
Digital divide

SMS and loudspeaker alerts, large text and Vietnamese labels.

6 · Journey map

What a working station changes

An illustrative scenario: Mrs Lan, 58, farms fish downstream on the Nhuệ in Thanh Trì.

02:00Discharge

Untreated water enters the river upstream of her ponds.

02:01Detected

A station sees oxygen fall for three readings in a row.

02:02Warned

She receives an SMS: do not pump river water tonight.

06:00Protected

She keeps the pond gates shut and her fish survive.

Day 2Followed up

An inspector’s sample goes to the laboratory to confirm the source.

7 · Recommendations

What should happen next

Confirm quotes, operator and payer

Fix the final parts list and who runs and pays for it before ordering.

Prove one station in the field

Test accuracy, fouling and battery life at one outfall before buying more.

Agree the legal role of the data

Work with the city on how alerts and laboratory results are used as evidence.

Test with older residents

Check SMS and loudspeaker alerts with people least likely to use the app.

Add ammonium sensing

Ammonium points directly to sewage, which the current probes show only indirectly.

Share and link the data

Publish quality-labelled data and combine it with the city’s river stations.
