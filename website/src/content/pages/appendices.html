Appendix E — cost ledger and assumptions

This cost-only appendix separates listings from analyst allowances, not supplier quotations or field results. The independent 32-task strict-90-day illustration is historical; this model uses 48 early two-monthly DO/turbidity tasks within 36 monthly field routes. Six testing rounds and 16 station checks are included in those visits. Hardware renewal figures are funding reserves, not failure predictions (DFRobot, n.d.-c, n.d.-d, 2026; FDI Care, n.d.; Lalamove Vietnam, 2024; Render, n.d.-a; Viễn Thông Tia Sáng, n.d.; Việt Nam Tái Chế, n.d.).
