HanoiWaterWatch

A live sensor network on Hanoi’s drain outfalls. Every discharge, seen within minutes.

Low-cost solar stations measure the outflow every two seconds and warn the city and nearby residents the moment untreated wastewater enters the river.

Our design questionHow might we detect untreated wastewater discharges into Hanoi’s rivers in real time?

Problem
Discharges are found days late, and the polluter cannot be traced.
Solution
Sensor stations at outfalls, a colour-coded map app and instant alerts.
Who benefits
Riverside residents, environment authorities, downstream farmers.
Pilot cost
About ₫41.7 million in listed equipment for three stations; installation and running costs to be quoted.
Status
Working simulated prototype; user testing in progress.

Background

Rivers as open drains

The Tô Lịch, Kim Ngưu and Nhuệ rivers carry the wastewater of a fast-growing city, from both homes and industry (Nguyen et al., 2018).

Factory discharges are linked to high organic pollution in the Tô Lịch (Nguyen, 2019). In 2024, wastewater from the Thanh Oai Industrial Cluster failed national standards and residents reported skin irritation (Dai Doan Ket, 2024).
