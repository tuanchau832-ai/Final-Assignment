The ideal state

What success looks like

Today

  • Discharges are found days later, if at all.
  • The polluting outfall is unknown.
  • Residents learn from the smell or dead fish.
  • Inspections are spread thin and arrive late.

With WaterWatch

  • A discharge is detected within minutes at the exact outfall.
  • An inspector is sent with a time and a place.
  • Residents are warned before they use the water.
  • Polluters pay, and open data builds trust.
